PROJECT LEDGER GLASS
CALL DATA ANALYSIS — SUMMARY
Reference: CDA-07
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL

1. MATERIAL REVIEWED

Call data records for four handsets across the period 1 December 2024 to 30 June 2025.
Content was not available. This analysis concerns pattern and timing only.

2. HANDSETS

H1 — +1-415-555-0288. Listed on the Ledger Glass Consulting incorporation filing and on
invoices issued by Lindqvist Build Group and Okonkwo Advisory. Used by Iris Delacroix.

H2 — handset attributed to Nadia Brandt, senior procurement officer.

H3 — handset attributed to Victor Lindqvist, principal of Lindqvist Build Group.

H4 — handset attributed to Selim Okonkwo, principal of Okonkwo Advisory.

3. FINDINGS

There is no direct contact between H2 and H3 in the reviewed period. There is no direct
contact between H2 and H4. There is no direct contact between H3 and H4.

H1 is in contact with all three.

H1 to H2: eighteen calls, clustered in the fortnight preceding each of the three award
dates. Twelve of the eighteen fall in a four-day window before an award. Mean duration
under ninety seconds.

H1 to H3: nine calls, including one on 11 January, the evening of a private dinner
booking made under the Delacroix name at which Lindqvist was a listed guest.

H1 to H4: five calls and eleven messages. The message pattern is less regular and is not
obviously tied to award dates.

4. INTERPRETATION

The procurement side and the vendor side of these transactions do not speak to each
other. Both speak to the same handset. On the call data alone, Ms Delacroix's handset is
the only communication path between them.

This is a structural observation, not a finding of wrongdoing. Call data establishes
contact, not content. No conclusion about the subject of any call can be drawn from this
material.

5. CAVEAT

Attribution of H2, H3 and H4 to named individuals rests on subscriber records and has
not been independently verified. Any handset may be used by a person other than its
registered subscriber.

END OF SUMMARY
