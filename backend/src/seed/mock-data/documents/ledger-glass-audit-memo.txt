1. PURPOSE

This vigilance memorandum records irregular procurement and money-routing patterns uncovered
during statutory audit of three municipal civil infrastructure contracts awarded between
January and June 2025, having an aggregate value of ₹142.5 Crore.

2. THE TENDER AWARDS

Tender 1 (PC-2025-01): ₹58 Crore (Flyover and Grade Separator Repair), awarded on 28 January to
Lodha Infracon Pvt Ltd. Two public sector competing bids were rejected during pre-qualification.

Tender 2 (PC-2025-04): ₹47.5 Crore (Smart Urban Corridor Sensor Network), awarded on 19 March to
Oswal Infrastructure Advisors LLP. Technical criteria weightage was altered post technical bid opening.

Tender 3 (PC-2025-09): ₹37 Crore (Comprehensive Drainage & Stormwater Overhaul), awarded on 20 June.
The Tender Evaluation Board was presided over by Standing Committee Chairman Harish Chandra Mehra.
Chief Engineer Neeta Bakshi signed the final sanction notes for all three contracts.

3. UNCOVERED CIRCULAR FINANCIAL ROUTING

Lodha Infracon Pvt Ltd and Oswal Infrastructure Advisors LLP have no declared common directors,
no shared corporate addresses, and no recorded joint ventures. Managing Director Varun Lodha and
Sanjay Oswal attended the same pre-bid conference at Taj Palace, Chanakyapuri, but maintain
distinct market profiles.

However, forensic bank statement analysis reveals both entities transfer heavy funds to a common
shell company: Kanch Consultancy Services LLP.

Lodha Infracon has executed 11 RTGS payments totalling ₹4.10 Crore to Kanch Consultancy Services LLP
for "Technical Feasibility Analysis". No project charter, DPR, structural calculation sheet, or site
inspection note is attached to any invoice.
Oswal Infrastructure Advisors LLP has made 6 bank transfers totalling ₹2.65 Crore using identical
invoice descriptions. Furthermore, an unapproved municipal payment of ₹68 Lakhs was routed to Kanch
LLP under a minor sub-contractor head.

All bank confirmations and communications are handled via accounts@kanchconsultancy.in and mobile
number +91-98100-55288.

4. THE INTERMEDIARY SHELL ENTITY

Kanch Consultancy Services LLP was incorporated on 2 November 2024 (LLPIN: AAD-4912).
Its sole Designated Partner is Isha Deshmukh (DIN 09283141). Its registered office at Suite 400,
91 Barakhamba Road, Connaught Place, New Delhi is a virtual address provider. A physical verification
conducted by Vigilance on 30 April confirmed no office, no staff, and zero operational infrastructure.

Isha Deshmukh is not named in any formal contractor bid and holds no official position in DMDA.
However, she is a co-trustee alongside Chairman Harish Chandra Mehra in Jan Seva Educational Trust.
Furthermore, banquet booking records show she hosted a private dinner on 11 January at Delhi Golf Club
where contractor Varun Lodha was in attendance — 17 days before Tender 1 was awarded.

5. FINDINGS

The contractors do not connect directly to each other; they route kickbacks through Kanch Consultancy
Services LLP. On the evidence available, Isha Deshmukh's shell entity represents the single structural
bridge between government decision-makers and winning private contractors.
