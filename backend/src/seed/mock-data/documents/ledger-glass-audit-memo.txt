PROJECT LEDGER GLASS
INTERNAL AUDIT MEMORANDUM
Reference: IA-33
Date: 6 May 2025
Author: Grace Tan, Internal Audit, Meridian Civic Contracts
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL

1. PURPOSE

This memorandum records a payment pattern identified during routine review of three
infrastructure tenders awarded between January and June 2025, with a combined value of
$18.6M.

2. THE AWARDS

Tender 1 (PC-2025-01), $7.2M, awarded 28 January to Lindqvist Build Group. Two of three
competing bids were withdrawn in the final week before submission.

Tender 2 (PC-2025-04), $5.9M, awarded 19 March to Okonkwo Advisory. The scoring sheet
records a late adjustment to the technical weighting, applied after bids had been opened.

Tender 3 (PC-2025-09), $5.5M, awarded 20 June. The evaluation committee was chaired by
Councillor Harold Meade. Nadia Brandt signed off all three awards in her capacity as
senior procurement officer.

3. THE PATTERN

Lindqvist Build Group and Okonkwo Advisory have no shared directors, no shared premises
and no recorded commercial relationship with each other. Victor Lindqvist and Selim
Okonkwo have both attended the same three pre-tender briefings, but nothing further
connects the two firms.

Both firms, however, make payments to the same entity: Ledger Glass Consulting.

Lindqvist Build Group has issued eleven payments to Ledger Glass Consulting totalling
$410,000, described as "advisory services". No scope of work, deliverable or timesheet
is attached to any of them. Okonkwo Advisory has issued six payments totalling $265,000,
using wording identical to the Lindqvist invoices. A single further payment appears to
have been routed to Ledger Glass Consulting through a subcontractor line item on a
Meridian Civic Contracts schedule; this one is less clear and should be confirmed before
it is relied upon.

All invoices are directed to a single mailbox, accounts@ledgerglass.example, and carry
the contact number +1-415-555-0288.

4. THE COUNTERPARTY

Ledger Glass Consulting was incorporated on 2 November 2024. Its sole named officer is
Iris Delacroix. Its registered address, Suite 400, 91 Chandler Street, is a
mail-forwarding suite; a site visit on 30 April found no office and no staff. The
company has no employees on record. The number +1-415-555-0288 appears both on the
Ledger Glass filings and on invoices issued by two of the vendors.

Ms Delacroix is not named in any complaint and holds no role at Meridian Civic
Contracts. She is recorded on the same charity board minutes as Councillor Meade in
2023, and appears as the host of a private dinner booking on 11 January at which Victor
Lindqvist was a listed guest — seventeen days before Tender 1 was awarded.

5. OBSERVATION

The three vendors do not appear connected to each other. They are connected to the same
intermediary. On the evidence available, Ledger Glass Consulting is the only point at
which the vendor side and the procurement side of these transactions meet.

6. RECOMMENDATION

Refer to external investigation. This memorandum does not conclude that any payment was
improper; it records that the pattern cannot be explained by the documentation held.

G. Tan
