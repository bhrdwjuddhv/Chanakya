Classification: CONFIDENTIAL

Referral received from Calderon Trust Bank concerning account activity on the business
account of Ledger Glass Consulting, held at the bank's Chandler Street branch.

ACCOUNT PROFILE

The account was opened on 8 November 2024, six days after incorporation. The sole
signatory is Iris Delacroix. The stated business activity is "management consultancy".
Declared expected turnover at opening was $80,000 per annum.

ACTIVITY

Actual credits in the eight months to 30 June 2025 total $743,000, roughly nine times
the declared expectation. Credits arrive from three sources: Lindqvist Build Group,
Okonkwo Advisory, and a single payment from a subcontractor line on a Meridian Civic
Contracts schedule.

Debits follow a consistent pattern. Within four working days of each credit, between 60
and 70 percent of the amount is transferred onward to a personal account held by
Iris Delacroix, and a smaller sum, typically 15 percent, is transferred to an account in
the name of Pelham Nominees Ltd, a company the bank has been unable to associate with
any trading activity.

A further recipient appears twice: an account in the name of H. Meade. The two transfers
total $48,500 and are described in the payment reference as "consultancy reimbursement".

NEW ENTITIES IDENTIFIED

Pelham Nominees Ltd — recipient of approximately $111,000 across the period. Registered
office given as 12 Rookwood Terrace.

Daniel Rehm — named as the second signatory added to the Ledger Glass account on
3 February 2025. Rehm does not appear on the incorporation filing and is not otherwise
known to this investigation.

BANK'S OBSERVATION

The account operates as a pass-through. Funds do not rest in it, no operating costs are
paid from it, and no payroll runs through it. The bank has restricted the account pending
the outcome of this referral.
