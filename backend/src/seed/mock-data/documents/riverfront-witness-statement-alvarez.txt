Statement of Sofia Alvarez, 29, of 4 Weir Court.

I run the riverfront most evenings, usually starting around seven o'clock from the
bridge end and going east along the walkway.

On Saturday 8 March I started a little later than usual, maybe five past seven. I passed
a woman walking east near the east reach. I recognised her from the running club — I
knew her as Alina. We nodded at each other. She was on her phone and she was walking
fast, faster than her usual pace. She looked like she was trying to get somewhere.

I did not hear what she was saying. I did not see anyone with her and I did not see
anyone following her.

I checked my watch shortly after passing her because I was behind my usual time. It was
ten past seven.

I carried on east as far as marker 8 and turned back. On the way back I did not see her
again. That would have been around half past seven. I do not remember any cars stopping,
but the walkway is set back from the road there and I had headphones in.

I did not know Alina well. We had spoken at the club a few times about training. I did
not know where she worked until I read about it afterwards.

I have not been shown any photographs and I have not been asked to identify anyone.

This statement is true to the best of my knowledge and belief.

S. Alvarez
