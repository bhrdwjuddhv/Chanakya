OPERATION HARBOR SHADOW
SURVEILLANCE LOG — CONSOLIDATED SUMMARY
Reference: HS-SURV-004
Prepared by: Det. D. Whitlock
Classification: RESTRICTED

1. SCOPE

This log consolidates static and mobile surveillance conducted between 14 January and
19 May 2025 in connection with seven cargo thefts from the bonded warehouse at Pier 14,
operated by Harbor Logistics Ltd.

2. SUBJECTS

Marcus Vale (47), sole director of Vale Freight Holdings. Known locally as "the
Harbourmaster". Vale was never observed handling cargo directly. On every night of
interest he was observed either at the Northside Salvage Yard on Northside Avenue or in
the black Audi sedan, plate KDP-8823, within two kilometres of it.

Dmitri Sokolov (39). Observed entering the Pier 14 rear loading bay on four occasions
outside scheduled operating hours. On 3 February at 02:15 the white Isuzu box truck,
plate WGT-4417, was recorded departing the rear bay. The same plate was captured by
automatic number plate recognition arriving at the Northside Salvage Yard at 03:05 the
same morning.

Elena Rask (52), proprietor of Rask Antiques, 22 Camden Row. Rask has no recorded
contact with Sokolov. Two intercepted messages sent from the prepaid handset
+1-415-555-0142 refer to "handing the load to E." The identification of "E" as Rask is
an inference and has not been corroborated.

Tommy Nguyen (28), night-shift clerk at Harbor Logistics Ltd since 2022. Nguyen was on
shift for six of the seven incidents. In interview on 2 April he admitted to two
meetings with Sokolov but denied knowledge of the thefts. Nguyen's handset,
+1-415-555-0197, is registered in his own name and was not used to contact any burner
number in the reviewed period.

Ray Okafor (34), contract driver. Okafor's delivery schedule places him at the Northside
Salvage Yard on three of the seven incident nights. He has held the keys to WGT-4417 on
at least one occasion, though it is not established that he drove it on 3 February.

3. HANDSET ACTIVITY

The prepaid handset +1-415-555-0142 first appears on the network on 11 March at 19:20,
near the Northside Salvage Yard. It is active only on nights of interest and is powered
off for the remainder of the period. Tower data for this handset mirrors Vale's known
movements on six of the seven nights. This correlation is strong but circumstantial;
the handset is not registered to Vale.

4. CORPORATE

Vale Freight Holdings and Northside Auto Salvage share a registered agent and file at
the same address. No common directorship has been established. Harbor Logistics Ltd is
the registered operator of the Pier 14 warehouse and is not itself a subject of this
operation.

5. WITNESS

Priya Chandra (41), logistics coordinator at Harbor Logistics Ltd, reported on 19 May
that six months of gate logs failed to reconcile with outbound manifests. Chandra works
overlapping shifts with Nguyen and knows him professionally. Her statement is recorded
at WS-07.

6. ASSESSMENT

The pattern is consistent with an organised crew operating with inside assistance. Vale
is assessed as the coordinating figure on the basis of position rather than direct
action: he is never present at the point of theft, but every route and every associate
passes through him.

END OF LOG
