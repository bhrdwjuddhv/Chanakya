1. SCOPE

This surveillance log consolidates static and electronic surveillance conducted between
14 January and 19 May 2025 in connection with seven high-value container cargo diversions
from the bonded CFS Yard 4 at JNPT Port, Nhava Sheva, operated by Nhava Sheva Freight & CFS Ltd.

2. SUBJECTS

Vikramaditya Singhania (47), Managing Director of Singhania Multi-Modal Logistics (Nariman Point).
Known on street wiretaps as "Bada Seth". Singhania was never observed handling physical cargo
or container seals. On every incident night, he was tracked at Kalamboli Steel & Scrap Yard
or travelling within two kilometres in his black Toyota Fortuner, registration MH-01-DE-8823.

Devendra Shukla (39), alias "Deva". Observed entering the JNPT CFS Yard 4 rear gate on four
occasions outside scheduled customs hours. On 3 February at 02:15 AM, the white Tata Prima
container truck, plate MH-06-BW-4417, was recorded departing the rear bay without manifest clearance.
The same plate was logged by Fastag sensors arriving at the Kalamboli Scrap Yard at 03:05 AM.

Esha Ray (52), proprietor of Ray Antiques & Curios, 22 Kala Ghoda, Fort, Mumbai. Ray has no recorded
direct communication with Shukla. Two intercepted messages sent from prepaid mobile +91-98201-55142
refer to "handing the high-value consignment to ER Fort gallery". The identification of "ER" as Esha Ray
is an intelligence inference awaiting corroboration.

Tanmay Nambiar (28), night-shift customs clearance clerk at Nhava Sheva Freight since 2022. Nambiar was
on roster for six of the seven diversion incidents. In inquiry on 2 April he admitted to two secret meetings
with Devendra Shukla at a Seawoods dhaba, but denied knowledge of container tampering. Nambiar's official
mobile, +91-98201-55197, is registered in his own Aadhaar name.

Raghav Ojha (34), contract heavy vehicle driver. Ojha's GPS trip sheets place him at the Kalamboli Scrap
Yard on three incident nights. He holds gate passes for MH-06-BW-4417, though it is not verified if he
was at the wheel on 3 February.

3. CELL TOWER ANALYSIS

The prepaid SIM +91-98201-55142 first activated on 11 March at 19:20 near Kalamboli Industrial Area.
It activates strictly during midnight container movements and is switched off during daytime hours.
Tower triangulation mirrors Singhania's movements across 6 of the 7 incident nights.

4. CORPORATE TIES

Singhania Multi-Modal Logistics and Kalamboli Scrap & Logistics Hub share the same registered chartered
accountant and file under identical corporate addresses on MCA-21. Nhava Sheva Freight & CFS Ltd is the
authorized custodian of the JNPT yard and not an accused entity.

5. WITNESSES

Priya Chandra (41), Customs Superintendent at JNPT. Reported the unmanifested container seal breakages
and initiated the internal customs audit.
