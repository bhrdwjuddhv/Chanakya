RIVERFRONT DISAPPEARANCE
MISSING PERSON REPORT — CONSOLIDATED NARRATIVE
Reference: MP-2025-0309
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL

1. SUBJECT

Dr. Alina Petrova, 36, senior researcher in composite materials at the Riverfront
Bioscience Institute, 3 Foundry Lane. Reported missing on 9 March 2025 by the Institute
director, Dr. Helena Fross, after Petrova failed to attend a scheduled review.

2. LAST KNOWN MOVEMENTS

Access control records show Petrova badging out of Institute Lab B through the north
door at 18:52 on 8 March. At 19:10 she was seen on the east reach of the Riverfront
Walkway by Sofia Alvarez, 29, who passed her walking east. Alvarez states the two
recognised each other from a running club and exchanged a greeting. She describes
Petrova as "in a hurry, on the phone".

Petrova's handset, +1-415-555-0331, registered a final tower connection on the east
reach at 19:26. There has been no network activity since. There is no CCTV coverage on
the walkway between markers 4 and 7.

At 19:31 a grey estate car passed the walkway camera at marker 3. The partial plate is
consistent with RTM-2290, a grey Volvo registered to Jonas Kerr. The driver is not
identifiable from the still.

3. ASSOCIATES

Jonas Kerr, 44, is a lab colleague of Petrova at the Institute. On 24 February Petrova
formally contested Kerr's sole authorship of a composite materials patent filing on
which she claims joint invention. The dispute was live at the time of her disappearance.
Kerr lives at 61 Alder Street, approximately 1.4 kilometres from the walkway. He states
he was at home from 18:00 on 8 March. No independent confirmation has been obtained.

Dr. Helena Fross, 58, is the Institute director. She reported the disappearance and has
cooperated fully. She confirms the patent dispute and describes relations between
Petrova and Kerr as "professionally strained but not hostile".

4. THIRD-PARTY ACCESS

The Institute's handwritten visitor log for 6 March, page 212, records an entry for
"M. Vale" signing into Lab B at 15:40. No badge was issued and no host is recorded. The
Institute's specimen transport contract has been held by Vale Freight Holdings since
2023. Vale Freight is directed by Marcus Vale, who is a named subject in a separate
matter, Operation Harbor Shadow. Whether the visitor log entry refers to the same
individual has not been established.

5. FINANCIAL AND DIGITAL

No activity on Petrova's accounts since 8 March. Her Institute mailbox,
a.petrova@riverfront-bio.example, shows no outbound messages after 17:44 that day. No
ransom or contact demand has been received.

6. CURRENT ASSESSMENT

Three lines of enquiry remain open: the patent dispute with Kerr; the unexplained
visitor entry connected to Vale Freight Holdings; and the possibility of misadventure on
an unlit stretch of walkway. No line is presently favoured over the others. There is no
physical evidence of assault and no body has been recovered.
